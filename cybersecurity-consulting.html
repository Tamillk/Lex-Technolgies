FAQ

A few common questions about cybersecurity consulting.

What is included in cybersecurity consulting?

Cybersecurity consulting can include architecture review, hardening priorities, cloud and IAM advisory, secure SDLC guidance, incident readiness planning, and security roadmaps aligned to engineering reality.

When should a team choose consulting instead of a penetration test?

Choose consulting when you need broader security guidance, architecture decisions, or program priorities. Choose penetration testing when you need exploit validation for specific applications, APIs, networks, or cloud attack paths.

Can cybersecurity consulting include ongoing advisory support?

Yes. Advisory support can be scoped as a focused sprint or ongoing engagement covering reviews, roadmap checkpoints, remediation guidance, and executive reporting.

Need a security roadmap with practical next steps?

Share your environment and current pain points. We will recommend the smallest consulting scope that gives you clarity and momentum.
