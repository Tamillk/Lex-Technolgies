Privacy Policy

This policy applies to our website and to information you share with us when you contact Lex Technologies.

1. Who we are

Lex Technologies ("Lex", "we", "us") is a cybersecurity company based in India. Our website is lexcyberwall.com.

2. Information we collect

We may collect the following categories of information:

  • Information you provide: your name, email address, company name, and the contents of your message when you contact us by email or through the website.
  • Technical and usage data: IP address, browser type, device information, referral URLs, and timestamps in standard server logs (typically collected by your hosting or network provider).

3. How we use information

We use information to:

  • Respond to inquiries, scope work, and provide cybersecurity services.
  • Maintain the security and availability of the website (for example, to detect abuse).
  • Comply with legal obligations and enforce our terms.

4. Cookies and tracking

We do not run advertising trackers on this website. If we add analytics or other tracking in the future, we will update this policy accordingly.

5. How we share information

We may share information only as needed:

  • With service providers (for example, hosting and email) to operate the website and communicate with you.
  • With professional advisors (lawyers, accountants) when necessary.
  • To comply with lawful requests or protect rights, safety, and security.

6. Client confidentiality

If you engage us for an assessment, your data is treated as confidential and handled under your contract and agreed scope. We do not publish client-sensitive information without permission.

7. Data retention

We keep inquiry and business communications as long as needed to respond, provide services, and meet legal obligations. Retention periods vary based on the nature of the relationship and applicable law.

8. Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information we handle. No system can be guaranteed 100% secure.

9. Your rights

Depending on your location, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or restrict processing of your personal information, and to object to certain processing. To make a request, contact us at contact@lexcyberwall.com.

10. International transfers

We may process information in India and other locations where our service providers operate. When required, we use appropriate safeguards for cross-border transfers.
