Methodology

We follow a pragmatic sequence that mirrors how real incidents happen.

01

Assess

We map the attack surface, validate risky paths, and identify what can actually lead to impact.

02

Harden

We prioritize fixes and remove fragile patterns across identity, apps, and cloud configuration.

03

Monitor

We improve signal: log coverage, alert tuning, and detections that your team can own.

04

Respond

We build playbooks and run drills so response is calm, fast, and coordinated.
